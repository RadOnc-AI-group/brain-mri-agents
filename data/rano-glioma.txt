RANO Criteria for Glioma

There are 4 levels to classify the progress/treatment response of gliomas.

Complete Response (CR): 
    (1) Complete disappearance of enhancing tumor volume 
    (2) No new lesions (either measurable or non-measurable)

Partial Response (PR): 
    (1) 65% or more decrease in total enhancing tumor volume 
    (2) No new enhancing lesions 
    (3) No progression of non-measurable enhancing lesions into measurable enhancing lesions

Progressive Disease (PD): 
    (1) 40% or more increase in total enhancing tumor volume 
    (2) Appearance of a new enhancing lesion 
    (3) Clear progression of non-measurable lesions into enhancing lesions

Stable Disease (SD): SD occurs when the patient does not qualify for SD, PR or PD and requires: 
    (1) No progression (40% or more increase in enhancing tumor tumor volume) 
    (2) No new enhancing lesions 
    (3) No progression of non-measurable enhancing lesions into measurable enhancing lesions

Legend: non-measurable lesion = enhancing tumor volume < 10 mm³, enhancing lesion: lesion with enhancing tumor core volume > 0
